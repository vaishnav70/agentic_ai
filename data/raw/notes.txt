RANKING METHOD
There is no official global ranking of men's perfumes. The ranks in this
dataset are a composite built by a web-search AI from several public
rankings and lists: a perfume ranks higher when more independent sources
list it and when it places higher in each. Notes, longevity and projection
come from public sources and are community-reported, not lab-measured.
